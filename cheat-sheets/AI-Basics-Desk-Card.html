Good answers from AI chat

Ask well. Refine it. Check it. Send it clean.

FirstDescribe the situation. Leave out names, customers, and part or lot numbers.
1

Ask

Four parts, one message
  • Context: what happened, and what you have
  • Audience: who the answer is for
  • Constraints: what to stay inside
  • Format: the shape you want back
  • Attach the source and have it quote the line it used
2

Refine

Keep the conversation going
  • Go wide, then different, then narrow
  • Ask for the weak spots, not a grade
  • Change one thing at a time: length, reader, or format
  • Say what it should leave alone
  • If it drifts, start a new chat with a short summary
3

Check

More at stake, more checks
  • Open the source it quotes
  • Check numbers and units against the spec
  • Ask again in a new chat and compare
  • Explain it back in your own words
4

Send

Make it easy on the reader
  • Lead with what happened and end with the ask
  • Cut what the reader doesn't need
  • Say where AI helped and what you checked
  • Send it when you'd sign it
