AI for Troubleshooting · Before you come

Five things to do before the session.

The whole session is hands-on, on your own laptop, working a made-up tool down through every step of an 8D. Gemini Enterprise if you have a seat; Copilot Chat works for every step.

1

Check your AI tool

Copilot Chat is enough for every exercise. The invite explains how to open it.

Gemini Enterprise is better for the data work: counts and checks need code that actually runs, and Gemini runs it. Seats cost money, so your manager approves them with a short justification. If you want one, ask at least two weeks before your session.

A justification you can adapt

I troubleshoot [tool downs, excursions, repeat alarms] about [N] times a month. Gemini Enterprise lets me analyze alarm and FDC exports with code that runs, check AI-written plans, and work 8Ds faster. I expect it to save about [X] hours per problem.
2

Sign in once, before the day

  1. Open Copilot Chat (or Gemini Enterprise) and start a chat.
  2. Upload any small CSV or text file and ask a question about it.

If any step fails, contact IT or Andrew before the session, not during it.

3

Bring your laptop

  • Charged, with the charger
  • Able to reach your AI tool from the training room network
  • Signed in to your SkyWater account
4

Download the case kit

Unzip AI-for-Troubleshooting-case-kit.zip from the invite to a folder you can find. It has the playbook with every prompt from the session, a worked example with your answers and the technicians' findings, and the case files: a brief, shift notes, an alarm history, a maintenance log, FDC data and limits, a change log, a manual excerpt, old lessons and an 8D template.

Every file is synthetic training data, safe to upload. Don't bring real data; we won't use it in class.

5

Have AI Basics fresh

This course builds on AI Basics and won't repeat it. You should be comfortable with:

  • The four-part question: context, audience, constraints, format
  • Giving it the source, and making it quote the line
  • The four checks: open a source, check the numbers, try again, explain it back

Haven't taken it, or it's been a while? Read the AI Basics desk card (one page).

★

Optional

Think of one real problem from the past year you'd like to rework with these tools. Bring the story, not the data. We'll use a few as examples in the open forum.

You'll use

Is / Is Not and the 8D, run as a loop. If you've worked a tool down or an 8D before, you're ready. New to 8Ds? You'll pick it up as we go.
