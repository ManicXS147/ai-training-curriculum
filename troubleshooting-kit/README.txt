AI FOR TROUBLESHOOTING · CASE KIT

SYNTHETIC TRAINING DATA. Made up for the AI for Troubleshooting course. Not from any real tool, vendor, lot, product or person.
Use it in Gemini Enterprise, the tool this course runs in.

Start with case_brief.txt. The playbook has every prompt from the session, in order.
The worked example has your answers for the interview, the engineer's choices for each cycle,
and what the technicians found. Everything the AI writes back is your own tool's output.

Files
  playbook.md            The 8D loop: every prompt, in order (Troubleshooting Playbook v0.6)
  worked_example.md      Your answers, the engineer's choices, the technicians' findings bank
  case_brief.txt         The situation and the ask
  shift_notes.txt        Pass-down notes from the etch area, unedited
  alarm_history.csv      Three weeks of alarms, every code, ETCH-04 and ETCH-05 (46 rows)
  maintenance_log.csv    PMs, work orders, parts and the checks recorded at sign-off
  fdc_summary.csv        Fault detection (FDC) sensor summary for every lot run, two weeks
  fdc_limits.csv         FDC warning and alarm limits for each chamber
  change_log.csv         Every logged change: PMs, parts, software, facilities
  manual_excerpt.txt     The chuck chapter of the (made-up) tool manual
  past_lessons.txt       Lessons learned from earlier problems
  8D_template.docx       The 8D your record maps onto
