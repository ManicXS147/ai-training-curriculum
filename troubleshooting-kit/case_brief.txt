CASE BRIEF: ETCH-04 B KEEPS GOING DOWN

Situation (Monday, Nov 9, 2026, 7:00 a.m.)
ETCH-04 chamber B is down again on "He backside flow high." It came back from a chuck PM on Friday
morning and has gone down repeatedly since. Each reset gets it running again for a few hours.
The night shift is sure the cause is the new helium MFC installed at the PM.

You are the engineer or technician who just picked this up. Your manager wants three things today:
  1. A clear problem statement and an Is / Is Not.
  2. The most likely cause, backed by data, before anyone orders parts.
  3. An 8D started, with containment.

What you have (all in this folder)
  case_brief.txt        This page
  shift_notes.txt       Pass-down notes from the etch area, unedited
  alarm_history.csv     Three weeks of alarms on every ETCH-04 and ETCH-05 chamber
  maintenance_log.csv   PMs, work orders, parts and the checks recorded at sign-off
  fdc_summary.csv       FDC sensor summary for every lot run, every chamber, two weeks
  fdc_limits.csv        FDC warning and alarm limits for each chamber
  change_log.csv        Every logged change: PMs, parts, software, facilities
  manual_excerpt.txt    The electrostatic chuck chapter of the tool manual
  past_lessons.txt      Lessons learned from earlier problems (use it in exercise 5)
  8D_template.docx      A blank 8D

Terms
  Tool down     The chamber is stopped by an alarm or interlock and can't run product
  Chamber       ETCH-04 and ETCH-05 each have two process chambers, A and B
  PM            Preventive maintenance
  ESC / chuck   Electrostatic chuck: holds the wafer by electrostatic force during etch
  He backside   Helium fed under the wafer to cool it against the chuck. A seal at the chuck edge
                keeps it there. "He leak" is the flow needed to hold backside pressure, in sccm:
                the higher it is, the more helium is escaping past the wafer or the seal.
  MFC           Mass flow controller: meters a gas flow
  FDC           Fault detection and classification: sensors logged on every run, with limits
  Interlock     A hard limit that stops the chamber (here, He leak at 4.0 sccm)
  Containment   Short-term action that stops bad product from moving on while you find the cause
