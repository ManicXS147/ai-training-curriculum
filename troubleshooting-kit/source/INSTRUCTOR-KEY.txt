THE ANSWER
  Cause: the chuck seal was not fully seated at reassembly. It held for the cold sign-off check and opened as
  the chuck heated and cycled. The He leak climbs run by run until the 4.0 sccm interlock trips. A reset
  (dechuck, rechuck, pump/purge) settles the seal for a while: the leak drops back to about 1.2-1.5 and climbs
  again, faster each time (10, 7, 6, 4, 3 runs). Confirm by inspection: the data points at the seal; only
  opening the chamber proves it.
  Escape: fdc_limits.csv has no He leak WARNING limit on the B chambers (ETCH-04 B and ETCH-05 B; "tool
  default, interlock only"). The A chambers warn at 2.0 sccm. A warning would have flagged Fri 15:50
  (lot L26-4698), 10.3 h before the first down, and 2.4 to 5.2 h before each later one.
  Second escape: the PM sign-off check is done cold. Crew A checked hot on 04A's Oct 19 PM; crew B checked cold.
  The procedure doesn't force it.
  Prior knowledge: past_lessons.txt lesson 2024-031 is the same failure on ETCH-02 A, with an open action
  (warning limits on every chamber, hot leak check in the ESC PM). Owner left in 2025.
