PROJECT BRIEF: A FASTER CAP NITRIDE

The ask (from your manager, Monday Nov 2, 2026)
PECVD-03 is the bottleneck tool for the new product ramp. Step 38, the cap nitride deposition
(recipe CAPNIT-POR), takes 60 seconds of deposition per wafer. Cut deposition time by at least
20 percent without breaking anything downstream. Two weeks. 25 wafers for experiments.

What done looks like
  1. A designed experiment (DOE) that fits the wafer budget
  2. A recommended recipe, backed by the DOE
  3. Confirmation runs that pass on BOTH chambers of PECVD-03 (A and B)
  4. A change request the change control board can approve

The film today (CAPNIT-POR on PECVD-03)
  Target thickness 3000 A. Deposition rate about 3100 A/min.
  RF power 500 W, pressure 4.0 Torr, SiH4 200 sccm, NH3 2000 sccm, spacing 400 mil, 400 C

Specs for the new recipe
  Deposition rate         Mean at least 3700 A/min (that is 20 percent faster than today)
  Uniformity (NU)         At most 2.0 % (1 sigma, 49-point map). Cpk at least 1.33
  Film stress             -250 to -50 MPa (compressive). Cpk at least 1.33
  Refractive index (RI)   1.98 to 2.02 at 633 nm. Cpk at least 1.33
  HF wet etch rate (WER)  Mean within 10 percent of today's (see TR-2023-007)
Cpk is checked for each chamber separately.

Terms
  PECVD     Plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition
  NU        Nonuniformity: thickness spread across the wafer, as 1 sigma in percent of the mean
  RI        Refractive index, a quick check of film composition (higher = more silicon-rich)
  WER       Wet etch rate in 100:1 HF, a check of film density and composition
  Spacing   Gap between the showerhead and the wafer heater, in mil (thousandths of an inch)
  DOE       Design of experiments
  Cpk       Process capability: distance from the mean to the nearest spec limit, in units of 3 sigma
  POR       Process of record, the recipe running today
