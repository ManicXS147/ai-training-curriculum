MEETING SUMMARY CHECKLIST · sample standard          [practice file · invented]

A meeting summary has:
1. A subject line with the meeting's name and date
2. Decisions made (or "No decisions")
3. Actions, each with ONE owner and a due date
4. Open questions, and who will answer each one
5. The next meeting's date
6. Under 150 words. Outcomes only, not who said what.
