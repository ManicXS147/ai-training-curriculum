AI BASICS · PRACTICE PACK · INSTRUCTOR KEY
Not for trainees. Keep it out of the zip you send with the invite.
All content is invented. The "good result" lines describe what to look for;
they are illustrations, not captured Copilot output.

BEFORE THE SESSION
- Send AI-Basics-practice-pack.zip with the invite, or put the folder on the
  shared drive and put the path on the whiteboard.
- Each exercise has a Floor and an Office file. People pick either; mixed
  tables are fine.
- Every hands-on slide ends with a Checkpoint click after the timer. Use it to
  catch the room up before moving on.

QUICK RECOVERIES (any exercise)
- Can't attach the file: paste the text instead.
- Pasted something real from work: that's fine in Copilot Chat (no content
  restrictions), but the pack file keeps the room's answers comparable.
- Long thread getting muddled: new chat, paste the notes again (move 6).
- Neighbor got a different answer: expected. Same question, different wording
  each time (check 3). The facts should match the notes; the wording won't.
- Finished early: run the other track's file.

SECTION 02 · Prompt A or B?  5:00
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: feel the four parts and one move change the answer.
Trap built in: the notes say "Vendor FSE maybe Thurs??" (Floor) and
"Go-live ... maybe moves to the 24th??" (Office). A model filling gaps with
what's likely often turns "maybe" into a firm fact.
Checkpoint (2 min in): "Who has Prompt B back? Hands up." Help anyone still
on step 1 paste the notes.
A good Floor result (illustration): ETCH-04 down since 01:40, alarm 2217,
vendor visit NOT confirmed; CVD-02 needs its 3rd seasoning wafer before
release; MET-01 IT ticket unknown / new tech needs vent sign-off / safety walk
volunteer. Coffee machine, WET-01, particle check and the delivery dropped.
A good Office result: Ask: help getting the IT account answer by Friday; at
risk: go-live 17th may slip to 24th (not decided); changed: badge and safety
training now day 1, 3 of 5 reps replied.
Debrief (Checkpoint click): "Did any version say the vendor is coming
Thursday, or that go-live moved to the 24th? The notes only say maybe. That's
the 15-versus-10 again, and it's exactly what section 03 catches." Step 3's
"write 'not in notes'" usually fixes it: point that out.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 06 · Brainstorm in three turns  4:00
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: the good ideas show up in turns 2 and 3.
Checkpoint (2 min): "Who's on turn 3?" Anyone stuck on picking a topic gets
the spare in Practice-Prompts.html.
Debrief: ask two people which idea they'd try and which turn found it. Most
will say turn 2 or 3.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 07 · Draft and shape  5:00
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: you bring the facts, it brings the structure; small edits shape it.
Traps built in: Floor notes say "check soon if it doesn't come back" (no
time). Office notes have the walkway action with no owner and "next meeting
(date?)". Drafts often fill these in: "check again after 15 minutes", "Shipping
lead to clear the walkway by the 31st", a made-up meeting date.
Checkpoint (2:30): "Hands up if you're on step 2." Anyone with no draft yet:
paste Prompt 1 for them.
Debrief (Checkpoint click): "Every fact in your draft should be one you gave
it. Did yours add a time, an owner, or a date? Circle it; that's what we
review next." Remind them to keep the chat open.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 08 · Review race  4:00
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: a first review that keeps your voice; the checklist is the source.
What the checklist should surface (illustration):
  Floor: no exact time for "check soon"; no owner or review date; who to call
  is vague ("equipment on-call if it's a hard down"); acronyms (OCAP) not
  spelled out.
  Office: walkway action has no owner or due date; next meeting date missing;
  the audit-frequency idea is an open question with nobody to answer it.
Checkpoint (2 min): "Who has a list back?" If a chat rewrote the draft
instead of listing problems, have them add "Don't rewrite it. Number each
problem and quote the line."
Debrief (Checkpoint click): first table to read out a problem they would
have missed wins. Then: "Would you sign it now?" Point out that the fixes
for "no owner" and "no date" need a person: the AI can't know them.
