06

Brainstorm in three turns

4 minutes

Use a small annoyance from your own week, with no names, customers or part numbers in it.

Stuck for a topic? · use the spare

Spare annoyance
Our weekly team meeting runs 20 minutes over and half the room isn't needed for most of it.

Turn 1 · go wide

Give me ten ideas to fix this: [your annoyance]

Turn 2 · go different

Five more, and make them a different kind from these. At least two should cost nothing.

Turn 3 · narrow

Pick the one that fits [your real constraint, e.g. no budget, 12 people]. What's its weak spot, and what's the smallest first step I could try this week?
