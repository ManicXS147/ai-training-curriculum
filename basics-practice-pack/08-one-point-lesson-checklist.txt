ONE-POINT LESSON CHECKLIST · sample standard         [practice file · invented]

A one-point lesson has:
1. A title that names the task
2. Who it is for
3. Why it matters: one line on what goes wrong if it's skipped
4. Numbered steps, 7 or fewer, each starting with a verb
5. Every time and number stated exactly (no "soon", no "a while")
6. The most common mistake, called out
7. Who to call if it doesn't work
8. The lesson's owner and its next review date

It fits on one page, in plain words, with every acronym spelled out once.
