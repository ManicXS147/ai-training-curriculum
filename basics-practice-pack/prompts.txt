SECTION 02 · Prompt A or B?  (5 minutes)
====================================================================
FLOOR: use 02-shift-notes.txt     OFFICE: use 02-project-notes.txt

Step 1 · new chat · Prompt A
  Turn these notes into a pass-down.
  (Office: Turn these notes into a status update.)

Step 2 · new chat · Prompt B
  FLOOR:
  Here are my shift notes. The reader is the next shift lead. Keep only open
  items and what changed. Three bullets, open items first.
  OFFICE:
  Here are my project notes. The reader is my manager. Keep only what changed,
  what's at risk, and what I need. Three bullets, the ask first.

Step 3 · same chat as Prompt B · add one move
  Use the same shape as this example. If my notes don't say something, write
  "not in notes" instead of guessing.
  (Paste 02-example-pass-down.txt or 02-example-status-update.txt under it.)

Then: which version would you actually send?

SECTION 06 · Brainstorm in three turns  (4 minutes)
====================================================================
Use a small annoyance from your own week, with no names, customers or part
numbers in it. Stuck? Use the spare: "Our weekly team meeting runs 20 minutes
over and half the room isn't needed for most of it."

Turn 1 · go wide
  Give me ten ideas to fix this: [your annoyance]
Turn 2 · go different
  Five more, and make them a different kind from these. At least two should
  cost nothing.
Turn 3 · narrow
  Pick the one that fits [your real constraint, e.g. no budget, 12 people].
  What's its weak spot, and what's the smallest first step I could try this
  week?

SECTION 07 · Draft and shape  (5 minutes)  · KEEP THIS CHAT OPEN
====================================================================
FLOOR: use 07-lesson-notes.txt    OFFICE: use 07-meeting-notes.txt

Step 1 · draft
  FLOOR:  Turn these notes into a one-point lesson for new techs.
  OFFICE: Turn these notes into a summary email to the people who were there,
          plus the shipping lead.
Step 2 · shape (two edits, one at a time)
  Shorter. Lead with the most common mistake.   (Office: Shorter. Actions first.)
  Write it for someone in their first week.     (Office: Write it so the shipping
                                                 lead, who missed the meeting,
                                                 knows what they're being asked.)
Step 3 · protect one line
  Keep my line about [pick one line you like] word for word. Change nothing
  else.
Finish: add your own last line, for example
  "Drafted with AI from my notes. I checked the steps against them."

====================================================================
SECTION 08 · Review race  (4 minutes)  · same chat as 07, or paste your draft
====================================================================
Step 1 · ask it to be the reader
  Read this as [a tech in their first week / the shipping lead]. List three
  problems. Quote the line each one is on. Don't rewrite it.
Step 2 · check it against the standard
  Here is our checklist. Which items are missing or thin in my draft? Don't
  rewrite it.
  (Paste 08-one-point-lesson-checklist.txt or 08-meeting-summary-checklist.txt.)
Step 3 · you decide
  Fix the one problem that matters most, yourself. Would you sign it now?
