SHARPEN A DECISION
Use it when you face a decision that lands on others.
Chats: A frame · B build (and fix) · C review · D case against · E announce
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1 · FRAME THE DECISION (chat A)
Condition: use when you face a decision that lands on others.
In class: skip this. decision_frame.txt and my_leaning.txt are its output.

COPY FROM HERE
<decision_talk>
[Talk freely: the decision, why now, the options you see, who it affects, and what you lean toward]
</decision_talk>
<inputs>
- decision_talk and my answers: free talk. Sort every specific I mention (a name, number, system, date or story) as BINDING, EXAMPLE, BACKGROUND or GUESS. BINDING: the decision must respect exactly this. EXAMPLE: one instance of a general need; restate it generally. BACKGROUND: true, but constrains nothing. GUESS: my estimate; it becomes an open item.
</inputs>
<goal>
Interview me, then translate my answers into Decision Frame v1.0, built to the frame standard, that a separate chat can analyze without talking to me. Success: every dimension is Clear or carried as an open item, every frame line meets the standard, and I confirm the frame.
</goal>
<dimensions>
1. Decision: the question, in one sentence.
2. Options: each option, including doing nothing.
3. Deadline: when it must be decided, and why.
4. Blast radius: every group, customer, commitment and function it lands on.
5. Undo cost: what undoing it would cost, and when undoing gets harder.
6. Criteria: what a good outcome looks like, and how it would be measured.
7. Leaning: which option I favor now, and why.
</dimensions>
<status_definitions>
Clear: an explicit answer with one reasonable reading.
Fuzzy: answered, but vague, conflicting, or open to two readings.
Unknown: not yet discussed, or I said I don't know.
</status_definitions>
<frame_standard>
F1 One fact per line.
F2 Every line quotes or cites my words; a line you inferred is an open item instead.
F3 Every criterion names how it would be judged met or not met.
F4 Options are distinct, and one of them is doing nothing or keeping things as they are.
F5 Every GUESS and every unresolved point is an OPEN-n item naming what would close it. Never write TBD.
F6 The leaning appears only in its own block.
</frame_standard>
<limits>
- Each round: the status of dimensions 1 to 7, the new specifics with a proposed sort, then up to 3 questions, each with options A, B, C and D ("None of these, or I don't know"). Options are distinct answers of comparable appeal.
- A specific stays unsorted until I confirm its sort.
- Exit: when all seven are Clear, or after round 5. Every remaining Fuzzy or Unknown becomes an open item.
</limits>
<output>
During the interview:
1. Status: dimensions 1 to 7, each Clear, Fuzzy or Unknown.
2. Specifics to sort: | Detail | Proposed sort |, or NONE.
3. Questions.
At the end:
1. Specifics: | Detail | BINDING / EXAMPLE / BACKGROUND / GUESS | Restated |
2. Decision Frame v1.0, dimensions 1 to 6: | Dimension | Answer | My words |
3. Open items: | ID (OPEN-n) | What is unresolved | What would close it |
4. Leaning, as its own block, so it can be left out of the brief.
5. Final line: "Reply Confirmed, or list the dimensions to correct."
</output>

2 · BUILD THE BRIEF (chat B, new)
Condition: use with a confirmed frame. Leave your leaning out.
In class: upload consolidation_plan.txt and decision_frame.txt, then paste.

COPY FROM HERE
<source_material>
[Upload or paste the plan, proposal or work the decision rests on]
</source_material>
<decision_frame>
[Upload or paste the confirmed Decision Frame, without your leaning]
</decision_frame>
<date>[Today's date]</date>
<inputs>
- source_material: the only source for claims, figures and assumptions.
- decision_frame: sets the question, options, blast radius, criteria and open items; adds no facts. Keep its OPEN IDs.
</inputs>
<goal>
Translate the source material into Decision Brief v1.0 that meets the standard, so I can decide with the evidence, assumptions and gaps in view. Success: every row passes DB1 to DB7.
</goal>
<standard>
DB1 [Lint] Every claim, figure and assumption row has an ID and a quote or NOT IN SOURCE.
DB2 [Review] Every claim the decision rests on is listed, with the evidence the source gives and where, or NO EVIDENCE.
DB3 [Review] Every figure is recomputed from the source; a mismatch shows both values.
DB4 [Review] Every assumption, stated or implied by the plan, is rated for impact and certainty with the anchors.
DB5 [Lint] Every option in the frame has a cell for every criterion in the frame.
DB6 [Review] Information that is absent and could change the decision is an open item, including anything the frame's blast radius or criteria need that the source does not cover.
DB7 [Lint] The brief contains no recommendation and no ranking of options.
</standard>
<limits>
- Labels: NO EVIDENCE (a claim with no supporting fact in the source); FIGURE FAILS (the recomputed value differs); CANNOT RECOMPUTE (the source lacks the inputs); NOT IN SOURCE (an assumption the plan relies on but never states); SOURCE TO OPEN (a document or person outside the source that would settle a row).
- Impact: H = the decision's goal fails if this is wrong; M = cost or schedule slips; L = minor rework.
- Certainty: H = the source gives evidence; M = stated without evidence; L = not stated or contradicted.
- Option cells: what the source says about that option on that criterion, with row IDs, or NOT IN SOURCE.
- Open items carried from the frame keep their IDs; new ones take the next unused number.
</limits>
<output>
Title line: Decision Brief v1.0
1. Claims: | ID (CL-n) | Claim | Quote | Evidence | Where | Label |
2. Figures: | ID (FG-n) | Quote | Stated | Recomputed | Label |
3. Assumptions, high impact and low certainty first: | ID (AS-n) | Assumption | Quote or NOT IN SOURCE | Impact | Certainty |
4. Options: | Option | one column per criterion | Row IDs it depends on |
5. Open items: | ID (OPEN-n) | Missing information | How it could change the decision |
6. Sources to open: | Source | Rows it would settle |
7. Change log: | Version | Date | Row ID | Rule | Change |, with one line for v1.0.
</output>

3 · REVIEW THE BRIEF (chat C, new)
Condition: use after the brief is built. Never in the chat that built it.
In class: upload consolidation_plan.txt and decision_frame.txt, paste this,
then paste your brief inside <artifact>.

COPY FROM HERE
<source>
[Upload or paste what the brief was built from]
</source>
<decision_frame>
[Upload or paste the confirmed Decision Frame, without your leaning]
</decision_frame>
<artifact>
[Paste the Decision Brief]
</artifact>
<standard>
DB1 [Lint] Every claim, figure and assumption row has an ID and a quote or NOT IN SOURCE.
DB2 [Review] Every claim the decision rests on is listed, with the evidence the source gives and where, or NO EVIDENCE.
DB3 [Review] Every figure is recomputed from the source; a mismatch shows both values.
DB4 [Review] Every assumption, stated or implied by the plan, is rated for impact and certainty with the anchors.
DB5 [Lint] Every option in the frame has a cell for every criterion in the frame.
DB6 [Review] Information that is absent and could change the decision is an open item, including anything the frame's blast radius or criteria need that the source does not cover.
DB7 [Lint] The brief contains no recommendation and no ranking of options.
Anchors. Impact: H = the decision's goal fails if this is wrong; M = cost or schedule slips; L = minor rework. Certainty: H = the source gives evidence; M = stated without evidence; L = not stated or contradicted.
</standard>
<gate>
Impact (I):
1 Cosmetic: wording only; meaning unchanged.
2 Local: one row is weaker than it should be; nothing depends on it.
3 Misleading: a figure, rating or status is unclear, and someone could act on it.
4 Wrong: a figure, quote or rating differs from the source, or an assumption is shown as a fact.
5 Missing or invented: something the decision depends on has no row, or a row has no source.
Ambiguity (A):
0 Two readers would read the row the same way.
1 Two readers would read it the same way with small differences.
2 Two readers could read it differently, or it can't be checked.
Patch when I is 4 or 5, or when I is 3 and A is 2. Keep in every other case.
</gate>
<inputs>
- source: the ground truth for facts and figures.
- decision_frame: the ground truth for options, criteria and blast radius.
- artifact: the brief under review.
- standard: rules marked Lint are mechanical; rules marked Review need judgment.
</inputs>
<goal>
Find every place the brief fails its standard against the source and the frame. Success: each failure has evidence and a corrected or added row, and every Review finding carries a gate result.
</goal>
<limits>
- Check Lint rules row by row; every Lint failure is patched without a score.
- Rate each Review finding with the gate; only Patch findings go in the patch block.
- Evidence is the quoted source or frame line, or MISSING plus what is missing.
- Keep existing IDs; number new rows from the next unused ID.
- List failures only. When nothing fails, output only: NO FAILURES.
</limits>
<output>
1. Lint failures: | Row ID | Rule | Evidence |
2. Review findings: | Row ID | Rule | Evidence | I | A | Gate |
3. Patch block: | Row ID | Rule | Corrected or added row, in full |
</output>

4 · CASE AGAINST (chat D, new)
Condition: use after the brief is fixed.
In class: upload my_leaning.txt, paste this, then paste your brief inside
<decision_brief>. Horizon: the end of Q3.

COPY FROM HERE
<decision_brief>
[Paste the fixed Decision Brief]
</decision_brief>
<leaning>[The option you favor and why, in your own words]</leaning>
<horizon>[When the outcome will be known]</horizon>
<inputs>
- decision_brief: the only source of facts.
- leaning: the position to test. Its reasons are claims to test, not facts.
- horizon: the time window for the pre-mortem.
</inputs>
<goal>
The strongest case against my leaning, built only from the brief, so I decide knowing its weakest points. Success: every point cites brief row IDs, and the result names the evidence that would change the decision.
</goal>
<limits>
- Cite brief row IDs for every point; label a point with no ID OUTSIDE BRIEF.
- When a reason in my leaning has no row in the brief, list it as UNTESTED.
- Case against: 200 words or fewer.
</limits>
<output>
1. Case against: numbered points, each with its row IDs.
2. Pre-mortem: three ways the leaning fails within the horizon, each with AS or OPEN IDs.
3. What would change the decision: | Evidence | Option it favors | How to get it |
</output>

CAPTURE IT · MEETING NOTES (one saved prompt)
Condition: use after any meeting where something was decided or assigned.
Record only in Teams, and say so before you start. Practise at home with
ops_review_transcript.txt.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

COPY FROM HERE
<transcript>
[Upload or paste the meeting transcript]
</transcript>
<meeting>
<purpose>[What the meeting was for, one line, or NOT STATED]</purpose>
<date>[Meeting date, with the weekday]</date>
</meeting>
<inputs>
- transcript: the only source for what was said; record only what it contains.
- meeting: background for headings, wording and dates only.
</inputs>
<goal>
Meeting notes every attendee can act on without rereading the transcript. Success: every decision, action and unsettled point in the transcript has exactly one item, each with its quote.
</goal>
<limits>
- Decisions: what was decided, who decided, and the condition or "None stated".
- Actions: one owner, one due date and one deliverable each, starting with a verb.
- Anything that waits on another approval, and anything raised but not settled, including brief concerns, is an open item.
- Dates: write each as a calendar date worked out from the meeting date, followed by the words used.
- Uncertainty labels: NOT STATED (never said; also add an open item), UNCLEAR (two readings; quote both), CONDITIONAL (waits on another approval or event).
- Names, numbers and examples appear only where they are part of a decision or action.
</limits>
<output>
1. Decisions: | ID (DEC-n) | Decision | Decided by | Condition | Quote |
2. Actions: | ID (ACT-n) | Action | Owner | Due | Deliverable | Quote |
3. Open items: | ID (OPEN-n) | Item | Raised by | Label | Quote |
4. Note to send, 250 words or fewer: decisions, then actions by owner, then what's still open.
</output>
COPY TO HERE
Read the notes against what you heard, then send them under your name.
Keep the transcript.

REQUIREMENT CARD (interview, then review)
Condition: use when a goal needs a tool that doesn't exist yet. New chat.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

COPY FROM HERE
<goal_statement>
[The goal or problem, in your own words]
</goal_statement>
<inputs>
- goal_statement and my answers: free talk. Sort every specific I mention (system, product, number, name) as BINDING, EXAMPLE, BACKGROUND or GUESS. BINDING: the tool must respect exactly this. EXAMPLE: one instance of a general need; restate it generally. BACKGROUND: true, but constrains nothing. GUESS: my estimate; it becomes an open item.
</inputs>
<goal>
Interview me, then translate my answers into Requirement Card v1.0 a builder can design from without talking to me. Success: every dimension is Clear or carried as an open item, every requirement meets the card standard, and I confirm the card.
</goal>
<dimensions>
1. Job: what it must answer, draft or decide.
2. Users: who uses it, and when.
3. Sources: the documents and data it needs.
4. Rules: limits on data and users.
5. Sign-off: who is accountable for its output.
6. Measure: what gets faster or better, and how we would know.
</dimensions>
<status_definitions>
Clear: an explicit answer with one reasonable reading.
Fuzzy: answered, but vague, conflicting, or open to two readings.
Unknown: not yet discussed, or I said I don't know.
</status_definitions>
<card_standard>
R1 Each requirement is one EARS statement: "When <trigger>, the <capability> shall <response>", or "The <capability> shall <response>" when it always applies.
R2 Singular: one "shall" per requirement.
R3 Verifiable: a test, demonstration or record can show it met or not met.
R4 Solution-free: it names a product only when that product is BINDING.
R5 Every requirement cites my words; an inference is an open item instead. Never write TBD.
</card_standard>
<limits>
- Each round: the status of dimensions 1 to 6, the new specifics with a proposed sort, then up to 3 questions, each with options A, B, C and D ("None of these, or I don't know").
- Exit: when all six are Clear, or after round 5. Every remaining Fuzzy or Unknown becomes an open item.
</limits>
<output>
During the interview:
1. Status: dimensions 1 to 6, each Clear, Fuzzy or Unknown.
2. Specifics to sort: | Detail | Proposed sort |, or NONE.
3. Questions.
At the end:
1. Specifics: | Detail | BINDING / EXAMPLE / BACKGROUND / GUESS | Restated |
2. Requirement Card v1.0: | ID (REQ-n) | Requirement | Dimension | My words |
3. Open items: | ID (OPEN-n) | What is unresolved | What would close it |
4. Final line: "Reply Confirmed, or list the IDs to correct."
</output>
