1. Each reason in the leaning is an unsupported assertion: half capacity (CL-1, FG-2), one team and one spares stock (CL-3), $1.2M a year (CL-4, FG-1, OPEN-1).
2. The plan contradicts itself: facilities work is said to fit a two-week shutdown but takes four weeks (FG-5, AS-9, OPEN-8). "Two tool sets" move, but the timeline moves one (FG-7, OPEN-9).
3. Line B fit is unproven: floor room only, no utility capacity (CL-2, AS-1, OPEN-3). Combined load cannot be computed (FG-2, AS-3).
4. Q3 cannot be checked, because there are no dates (CL-5, FG-6, AS-10, OPEN-5). The customer audit falls one week after full production (AS-4).
5. The 92% uptime has no data behind it, and it is assumed to hold under added load (CL-7, FG-3, AS-6, OPEN-4).
6. The cost is missing, so "worth it" cannot be judged (OPEN-2, AS-13). Savings also require Line A costs to disappear (AS-2). Reversal cost is unknown (OPEN-10).
7. Staffing and customer acceptance are assumed (CL-8, AS-7, AS-5, AS-12, OPEN-6, OPEN-7).
